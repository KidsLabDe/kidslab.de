Your name in 3D — you can actually touch it! In this project, you'll design your own nameplate with TinkerCAD and then print it out on a 3D printer. You can hang the plate on your bedroom door, put it on your desk, or give it as a gift.

## What is TinkerCAD?

TinkerCAD is a **free 3D program** that runs right in your browser. You don't need to install anything! You combine simple shapes (cubes, cylinders, spheres...) into your own design. Perfect for beginners!

## What you need

- A **computer or laptop** with internet access
- A **browser** (Chrome, Firefox, Edge)
- A free account at [tinkercad.com](https://www.tinkercad.com)
- Optional: access to a **3D printer** to print it out

## Step 1: Launch TinkerCAD

1. Go to **[tinkercad.com](https://www.tinkercad.com)**
2. Sign in (or create a free account)
3. Click **"Create new design"**
4. You'll now see the workspace — a blue grid with a panel on the right side

## Step 2: Build the base plate

Your nameplate needs a plate as its base:

1. Drag a **box** from the panel on the right onto the workspace
2. Click the box and change the dimensions:
   - **Width:** 80 mm (or more, depending on the length of the name)
   - **Depth:** 25 mm
   - **Height:** 3 mm
3. That's now your base plate!

{{< callout type="info" >}}
**Tip:** you can type the dimensions directly into the white fields when you click on an edge.
{{< /callout >}}

## Step 3: Add your name

Now for the fun part:

1. On the right in the panel, under **"Text,"** you'll find a 3D letter block
2. Drag it onto the workspace
3. Click on the text and type in **your name**
4. Adjust the size:
   - **Height:** about 5 mm (how tall the letters stand above the plate)
   - **Font size:** adjust so the name fits on the plate
5. Move the text **onto the base plate** — it needs to sit on top

{{< callout type="warning" >}}
**Important:** the text needs to **touch or slightly overlap** the base plate so both become one piece when printed. Use the side view to check this!
{{< /callout >}}

## Step 4: Combine everything

1. Select **all parts** (Ctrl+A or Cmd+A)
2. Click **"Group"** (the icon with the square at the top of the panel, or Ctrl+G)
3. Now the base plate and text are a single object — ready to print!

## Step 5: Add extras (optional)

Make your nameplate even more special:

- **A hole for hanging:** drag a cylinder onto the plate, turn it into a "hole" (click "Hole"), and group it. This creates a hole you can thread a cord through.
- **A frame:** add thin boxes along the edges of your base plate
- **Symbols:** in the library on the right you'll find stars, hearts, and other shapes
- **Rounded corners:** use a "rounded box" instead of a regular box

## Step 6: Export and print

1. Click **"Export"** at the top right
2. Choose **STL** as the format
3. Download the file
4. Open the STL file in a **slicer program** (e.g. Cura or PrusaSlicer)
5. Off it goes to the **3D printer**!

## Done!

You've designed your own 3D nameplate! That's a great first project to get to know TinkerCAD. If you enjoyed this, why not try our other TinkerCAD projects next!
