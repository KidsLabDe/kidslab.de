> Coding, robot building, 3D printing — our courses and open workshops at KidsLab Augsburg.

## How we work

> *"Help me to do it myself."* — Maria Montessori

- **Doing instead of watching** — a short intro, then straight to work. The kids build, code, and experiment from the very start
- **As a team** — the best ideas happen together
- **A presentation at the end** — everyone shows what they've built. It builds confidence and pride
- **All open source** — all materials and tools are freely available. What starts in the course can be continued at home
