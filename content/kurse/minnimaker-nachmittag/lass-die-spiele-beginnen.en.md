In our 4-part creative course, kids (aged 6 to 12) develop their own card game – inspired by well-known games like UNO or similar card game classics.

First, kids design their ideas the classic way, with pen and paper: they draw characters, symbols, and card motifs. Then the drawings are transferred digitally into Canva and further edited, designed, and combined there. This way, kids playfully learn to use digital design tools and experience how an idea becomes a finished game.

### Course details

- **Start:** Monday, March 2, 2026
- **Duration:** 4 sessions, 2 hours each
- **Time:** 4:00–6:00 pm
- **Dates:** each Monday, March 2–23, 2026
- **Location:** KidsLab, Herrenhäuser 17 at Fischertor

### What can kids expect?

- Drawing their own cards and motifs
- Introduction to digital design with Canva
- Developing creativity and imagination
- Creating their own card game step by step
- Playful learning of analogue and digital design
