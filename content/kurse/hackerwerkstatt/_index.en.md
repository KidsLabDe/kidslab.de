> Understand technology, don't just use it – the format for anyone 12+ who wants to go deeper.

**Hacker Workshop 12+** is for teenagers who want to know how technology really works. This isn't about gaming, it's about building, programming, and tinkering – guided by our team, step by step.

Hacker Workshop runs in **series of 4–6 weeks**, each with its own topic. That way, with every series you build new skills — from hardware to AI to web development.

**No prior knowledge needed** – just curiosity and a love of technology.

- **Age:** 12 to 18
- **When:** Fridays, 3:00–4:30 pm
- **Where:** KidsLab Augsburg, Herrenhäuser 17 at Fischertor
- **Materials:** everything provided on site, feel free to bring your own laptop
- **Cost:** €120 per series (reduced €60, free on request to gregor@kidslab.de). More info under [course fees and discounts](/ueber-kidslab/kursgebuhren-und-ermassigungen)
