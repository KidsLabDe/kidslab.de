## GamesPreis 2026 — The winners

On March 12, 2026, the best games of the 3rd Augsburger GamesPreis were honoured at the Kleiner Goldener Saal in Augsburg. 500 students from 20 classes had developed their own computer games with Scratch in one-day workshops.
