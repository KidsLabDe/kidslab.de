<h2>What is GamesLab?</h2><p>GamesLab is an idea and initiative by KidsLab. In GamesLab, kids, teenagers, and students learn to code in a playful way through computer games. More information is available at <a href="HTTPS://Kidslab.de/GamesLab">HTTPS://Kidslab.de/GamesLab</a></p><h2>Why the handbook?</h2><p>During the three-hour workshop with school classes, students learn the basics of game design as well as an introduction to the visual programming language Scratch. Of course, that's not the end of it, and we want to encourage you to keep working on your games — ideally, you'll even submit them to the GamesPreis. The handbook is here to support you along the way.</p><h1>Handbook for students</h1><h2>PDF version, with layout (in German)</h2><p><a href="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/KidsLabDe/GamesLab-Handbuch/main/pdf/GamesLab%20Handbuch.pdf">Handbook, single pages</a></p><p>[Handbook, spreads](<a href="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/KidsLabDe/GamesLab-Handbuch/main/pdf/GamesLab">https://raw.githubusercontent.com/KidsLabDe/GamesLab-Handbuch/main/pdf/GamesLab</a> Handbuch doppelseiten.pdf)</p><h2>eBook – ePUB version for e-readers (iPad etc.)</h2><p><a href="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/KidsLabDe/GamesLab-Handbuch/main/GamesLab-Handbuch.epub">Handbook, eBook</a></p><h2>Word</h2><p><a href="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/KidsLabDe/GamesLab-Handbuch/main/GamesLab-Handbuch.docx">Handbook, Word</a></p><h1>Teacher's handbook</h1><p>In addition to the handbook for students, there is also a dedicated handbook that prepares the running of GamesLab and its many examples for classroom use.</p><p><a href="https://github.com/KidsLabDe/GamesLab-Handbuch/raw/main/Lehrerhandbuch/GamesLab-Handbuch-Lehrkr%C3%A4fte.pdf">Teacher's handbook, PDF</a></p><p><a href="https://github.com/KidsLabDe/GamesLab-Handbuch/raw/main/Lehrerhandbuch/GamesLab-Handbuch-Lehrkr%C3%A4fte.epub">Teacher's handbook, eBook</a></p><p><a href="https://github.com/KidsLabDe/GamesLab-Handbuch/raw/main/Lehrerhandbuch/GamesLab-Handbuch-Lehrkr%C3%A4fte.docx">Teacher's handbook, Word</a></p><p><em>Note: the handbooks themselves are currently only available in German.</em></p><h1>Sharing and passing it on</h1><p>Sharing & building on it welcome!
