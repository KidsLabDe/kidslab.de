## The complete package for your classroom

### 1. Workshop at your school

We come to you with our team and all the equipment. A GamesLab workshop lasts one school morning (approx. 3–4 hours). Students learn:

- **Game design** — what makes a good game? Storytelling, game mechanics, level design
- **Programming with Scratch** — visual, browser-based, no installation needed
- **Teamwork** — playable prototypes emerge in small teams

By the end of the day, every team has its own game — and the experience that programming has nothing to do with boredom.

### 2. Handbooks for teaching and follow-up

Our didactic **teacher's handbook** prepares the workshop content for regular classroom use. The **student handbook** supports independent continued work. Both handbooks are **open source** (CC BY 4.0) — copying, adapting, and sharing is explicitly encouraged.

### 3. GamesLab Studio — the platform

After the workshop, your students can keep working independently on [GamesLab Studio](/gameslab/studio/):

- **Emoji login** — no password, no email. 5 emojis are enough.
- **Challenges** — structured programming tasks with increasing difficulty
- **XP & leaderboard** — motivation through gamification
- **Manage classes** — you keep track of progress

The Studio is free, browser-based, and GDPR-compliant — no personal data needed.

### 4. Training & workshops

On request, we offer training for your staff:

- **Scratch in the classroom** — an introduction to visual programming
- **Game design as a method** — game development as a classroom project
- **Using GamesLab Studio** — setting up the platform, creating classes, assigning challenges
