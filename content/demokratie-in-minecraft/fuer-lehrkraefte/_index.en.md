## The two formats at a glance

### Digital Future Days (current, since 2025/26)

The newer day format runs from **8 am to 4 pm** and is structured as a **four-part concept**:

1. **Digital Future Day** at the school — an input phase on local politics, then a creative phase in Minetest
2. **Classroom lesson** — deepening the topics of political levels and participation (materials from the BLZ)
3. **Extracurricular meeting** — students from different schools in a municipality work together with the Bavarian Youth Council to develop their project ideas further
4. **Discussion event** — presentation to representatives of local politics

{{< callout type="info" >}}
A collaboration between the [Bavarian State Centre for Civic Education](https://www.blz.bayern.de), the [participation unit of the Bavarian Youth Council](https://www.bjr.de), [KidsLab](https://kidslab.de), and [Q3 – Quartier für Medien.Bildung.Abenteuer](https://www.qdrei.info/).
{{< /callout >}}

### Digital Future Nights (original format, 2022–2024)

The overnight format runs from **4 pm to 10 am the next morning**. Students stay overnight at the school and work on their Minetest projects all night. This format was launched during the "Bavarian Year of Youth 2022" and ran at over 27 schools across every Bavarian school inspectorate district.

## Schedule of a Digital Future Day

| Time | Content |
|---|---|
| 8:00–8:10 am | Welcome, introductions, warm-up |
| 8:10–8:40 am | Short workshop: how does local politics work? |
| 8:40–9:10 am | Future workshop: my municipality today and tomorrow |
| 9:10–9:20 am | Introduction to Minetest |
| 9:20–9:55 am | First part of the creative phase |
| 9:55–10:10 am | Break |
| 10:10–11:55 am | Creative phase in Minetest with feedback rounds |
| 11:55 am–1:00 pm | Lunch break |
| 1:00–3:15 pm | Independent work in Minetest |
| 3:15–3:45 pm | Presentation of results |
| 3:45–4:00 pm | Evaluation and outlook |

## What makes this project special?

- **Experiencing local politics first-hand** — enabling a real sense of self-efficacy
- **Breaking down barriers** between politics and school
- **Statewide in Bavaria** — including especially in rural areas
- **Strong connection to everyday life** — the students' own municipality takes centre stage
- **Methodological innovation** — civic education meets media education

## Tasks for the school

{{< callout type="warning" title="Checklist" >}}
The complete checklist with all details can be found in the guides above.
{{< /callout >}}

- Register it as a school event
- Provide **laptops and mice** (one device per student)
- Install **Minetest** on all laptops (free, [minetest.net](https://www.minetest.net/))
- Provide **Wi-Fi login details**
- Inform parents/guardians
- Obtain photo consent forms (template in the guide)
- Organise catering (rule of thumb: €15–20 per person, costs reimbursed by the BLZ)
- Publicity for the project (press release template in the Future Days guide)

## Results: what has happened so far?

Many ideas from the future workshops have already become reality:

- Students from **Schweinfurt** took over their town's Instagram channel
- Teenagers from **Regenstauf** successfully campaigned for their own youth parliament
- In **Wurmannsquick**, a bouldering wall was built on the school grounds

All projects and future contracts are documented at [zukunftsnacht.de](https://zukunftsnacht.de/).
