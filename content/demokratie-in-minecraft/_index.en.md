> Imagine you could reinvent your town. Design every square, every street, every meeting spot exactly the way you want it. That's exactly what we do — in Minecraft!

With "Democracy in the Minecraft World," we combine gaming with real political participation. Teenagers don't just build virtual worlds — they develop concrete ideas for a better way of living together, and discuss them directly with local politicians. Pixels become plans, and gaming fun becomes democratic co-creation.

## The concept

86% of young people in Germany view the future with concern. Only 23% believe we'll get climate change under control. Less than a third feel they can help shape politics. We want to change that!

In our **future workshops**, teenagers from grade 8 upward explore their visions for their own municipality and creatively implement them using the open-world game [Minetest](https://www.minetest.net/) (the open-source alternative to Minecraft). Participants:

- **explore their town** and gather ideas for improvement
- **build their concepts** as 3D models in Minetest
- **discuss with politicians** as equals
- **sign future contracts** — concrete agreements with mayors about next steps

What makes it special: here, every voice really counts. Classic public participation formats barely reach young people — but in Minetest, instead of abstract wish lists, visualised concepts emerge that relate to real local places.

## From idea to reality

Since 2022, we've run over **50 future workshops** in **47 Bavarian municipalities** — from Munich and Augsburg to Berching and Waldsassen. Over **200 concrete project ideas** have emerged as a result. And many of them have already become reality:

- Students from **Schweinfurt** took over their town's Instagram channel
- Teenagers from **Regenstauf** successfully campaigned for their own youth parliament
- In **Wurmannsquick**, a bouldering wall was built on the school grounds

## The story: from Future Nights to Future Days

### 2022 — The beginning

The [Bavarian State Centre for Civic Education](https://www.blz.bayern.de) launches the **Digital Future Nights** as part of the "Bavarian Year of Youth." The original format: teenagers stay overnight at their school and spend the whole night building their Minetest projects (4 pm to 10 am the next morning).

### 2022–2024 — 27 Future Nights across Bavaria

Future Nights take place at Realschulen and Mittelschulen across every Bavarian school inspectorate district, run by [KidsLab](https://kidslab.de) and [Q3 – Quartier für Medien.Bildung.Abenteuer](https://www.qdrei.info/).

### 2025/26 — Evolving into Future Days

The format evolves into a **project day**: the [Digital Future Days](zukunftstage/) run during the day (8 am–4 pm) and are expanded with a classroom lesson, an extracurricular preparation meeting, and discussion events. New partner: the [Bavarian Youth Council](https://www.bjr.de).

## The Future Museum

The results of the future workshops can also be visited virtually: in the **Future Museum** in Minetest, the teenagers' visions are accessible as a walkable exhibition — around the clock, free of charge.

1. Install [Minetest](https://www.minetest.net/) (free, open source)
2. Connect to the "Zukunftsnacht Museum" server (password: `zukunft`)
3. Explore!
