## Learn to code in Minecraft – online course starting January 12, 2027

You love playing Minecraft and want to know how to write your own programs? In this course you'll learn real programming, right inside the Minecraft world. You'll write code for our robot turtle Robokröti and teach it to build, dig and solve problems for you.

You don't need any programming experience. You should enjoy Minecraft, though, and already know your way around the Java Edition a little.

## How the course works

**Live stream:** Once a week we meet for a live stream (60 minutes). We explain the new topic and put it into practice together right away on our server.

**TurtleCity:** On our programming server TurtleCity you can try everything out yourself, build your own projects and check out what others have programmed. TurtleCity is open daily from 2 pm to 6 pm.

**Videos and PDFs:** Every topic is also available as a video tutorial and as a PDF with the programs and explanations. If you miss a session, you can catch up at your own pace.

**Help:** If you get stuck, we'll answer your questions by email, WhatsApp or in the forum.

## Dates

- 8 live sessions, always on Tuesdays from 6:00 to 7:00 pm
- January 12, 19 and 26, February 2, 9, 16 and 23, and March 2, 2027

Our participants come from all over Germany, so the course also continues during school holidays. If you can't make it during the holidays, just catch up with the video.

## What you need

- a PC or laptop (Windows, Mac or Linux)
- Minecraft Java Edition (licence approx. €24, not included in the course price)
- our course modpack for the robot turtle – you'll get installation instructions before the start

## Who is it for?

For kids and teens aged 10 to 16.

## Safety

All online sessions are password-protected, and only course participants are on TurtleCity.
