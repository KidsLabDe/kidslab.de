> Kids and teenagers channel their fascination with computer games and create their own games with Scratch in no time at all!

Kids and teenagers are fascinated by computer games! That motivation can be put to use: make your own game! Scratch offers a very low barrier to entry, and with plenty of creativity and imagination, students create their own games in no time at all!

Scratch can be used across many subjects — not just computer science!

- **Target group**: primary school, secondary schools up to grade 8
- **Duration**: from 4 hours
- **Equipment**: tablets, Wi-Fi with internet access
