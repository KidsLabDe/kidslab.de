> With TinkerCAD and a 3D printer, kids and teenagers can design their own objects and print them for real!

With the free online program TinkerCAD, kids and teenagers can take their first steps in CAD design. It's very easy to use, and after 1–2 hours the first self-designed 3D object is ready on screen.

And the best part: the design can then be printed on a 3D printer, admired for real, and shown off!

- **Target group**: from grade 3 onwards, secondary schools
- **Duration**: from 4 hours
- **Equipment**: computers or tablets, internet, 3D printer
