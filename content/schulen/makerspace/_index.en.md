## What is a MakerSpace?

A MakerSpace (also known as a creative workshop, FabLab, or tinkering lab) is an open learning space where kids and teenagers work with technology, design, and digital tools. Typical MakerSpace activities at school:

- **3D printers** – design your own objects on a computer and print them
- **Robots & electronics** – Calliope mini, LEGO Technic, soldering
- **Coding** – from ScratchJr to Python
- **AI & digital media** – understanding algorithms, developing games

What makes it special: with making, there are no right or wrong answers – it's about **trying things out, failing, and thinking further**. That's exactly what strengthens creativity, teamwork, and problem-solving skills.

A MakerSpace doesn't have to be a luxury. Schools in Augsburg and Bavaria have good funding options:
