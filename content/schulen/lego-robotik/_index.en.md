> With LEGO Education Spike Prime, students invent, build, and program their own robot in just one morning!

With LEGO Education Spike Prime, students from primary school age invent, build, and program their own robot in just one morning! The intuitive sets give girls and boys a very easy way into mechanics, robotics, and programming.

By the end of the day, individual, working robots are standing in the classroom and driving around on their own!

- **Target group**: from grade 3 onwards, secondary schools
- **Duration**: from 2 hours
- **Equipment**: LEGO Education Spike Prime kits and tablets
