## AI Workshop: Robot Teachers Take Over?

Our workshop ["Robot Teachers Take Over?"](../ki-robolehrer/) is completely **open source** and can be run by any teacher themselves. Students engage critically with AI in a school context through role-play and perspective-taking.

- No special tech needed
- Approx. 3 class periods
- All materials freely available
