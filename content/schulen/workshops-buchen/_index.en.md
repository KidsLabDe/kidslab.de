> We come right to your school – with all materials, equipment, and mentors.

## How it works

1. **Choose a workshop** – browse our [offers](../)
2. **Get in touch** – write to us, we'll sort out the date and details
3. **We come to you** – we bring all materials and mentors

## 50% discount available

Thanks to the support of our sponsors and partners, schools can also book all offers at a **50% discounted price**. Just get in touch with us – we'll find a solution so that every school really does have access to our offers.

## Funding via Schule+

Many of our workshops can be funded through the **Schule+** programme. More information at [kulturkiesel.de/schuleplus](https://kulturkiesel.de/schuleplus).
