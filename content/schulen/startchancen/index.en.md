## Funding through the Startchancen programme

The Startchancen programme (running 2024–2034) provides additional funding to schools in challenging circumstances. For external partners like KidsLab, **Pillar II (Chancenbudget)** is relevant:

- **Who decides?** School leadership decides independently based on need — Bavaria does not prescribe a binding catalogue of measures.
- **How much budget?** On average around **€82,000 per school per year** (Pillar II Chancenbudget).
- **How is it billed?** Via a fee or service contract; state schools process payments through the **at:las** portal of the Bavarian State Office for Schools.
- **How many schools?** In the city of Augsburg alone, **25 schools** take part, around **580 schools** across Bavaria.

{{< callout type="info" >}}
**Not part of the Startchancen programme?** No problem. KidsLab workshops can also be booked at a **50% discount** through the **Schule+** funding programme — or paid for directly from the school budget.

## Our experience at Startchancen schools

KidsLab is already working successfully with Startchancen schools in Augsburg. Three examples from practice:

### Drei-Auen primary school, Augsburg-Oberhausen

The Drei-Auen primary school has been part of the Startchancen programme since 2024. KidsLab runs a **tech club** there that keeps evolving from term to term:

- **Phase 1:** introduction to programming with **Scratch** — own games and animations, no prior knowledge needed
- **Phase 2:** game development in **GamesLab** — one participant won a **category prize** at the Augsburger GamesPreis (presented by Digital Minister Mehring)
- **Phase 3:** robotics with **LEGO Spike Prime** — building your own robots, connecting motors and sensors, controlling them by program
- **Phase 4 (current, 2025/26 school year):** **stop-motion videos** — students produce their own animated films, combining creativity, technology, and storytelling

> "The enthusiasm was immediately noticeable: as soon as the parts were on the table, kids were already building, plugging in, and testing."

## Frequently asked questions

**What can I fund with the Chancenbudget of the Startchancen programme?**

The Chancenbudget (Pillar II) funds extracurricular offers, clubs, workshops, and cooperations with external partners. School leadership decides independently — Bavaria doesn't prescribe a fixed catalogue. The average budget is around €82,000 per school per year. Coding and maker workshops from KidsLab are a typical example of a fundable measure.

**Who decides on commissioning external providers?**

School leadership decides independently based on need. Unlike, say, Baden-Württemberg or Berlin, Bavaria does not prescribe a binding catalogue of measures. That's deliberate: schools should choose what fits their own situation.

**How do I find external partners for the Chancenbudget?**

Via the **Startchancen noticeboard** in the Bavarian school portal (km.bayern.de), through direct recommendations from other schools, or through internet research. KidsLab is listed on the noticeboard and available for direct inquiries.

**Is KidsLab only for Startchancen schools?**

No — KidsLab offers workshops for all schools in Augsburg and Swabia. For schools without Startchancen status, there's the **Schule+** programme (50% funding) or regular booking. We're happy to advise on funding options.

**Which workshops are suitable for primary school?**

From grade 1: Robot Mouse (coding without a screen), ScratchJr/OctoStudio (stories and games). From grade 3: LEGO Robotics with Spike Prime, 3D printing & CAD with TinkerCAD. All materials are brought along.

**How many Startchancen schools are there in Augsburg and Bavaria?**

In the city of Augsburg alone, 25 schools take part in the Startchancen programme (2024/25 and 2025/26 cohorts). Across Bavaria: around 580 schools, of which about 60% are primary and special-needs schools.
