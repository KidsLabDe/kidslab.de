> How can artificial intelligence take work off teachers' plates and make lessons more individualised? Hands-on, legally sound, and motivating.

How can artificial intelligence take work off teachers' plates and make lessons more individualised? In our interactive workshop, we show how AI can be used meaningfully in everyday school life — hands-on, legally sound, and motivating.

**What to expect:**

- AI tools for lesson planning and delivery
- Approaches to personalised learning
- Ethical & legal basics (GDPR, transparency, responsibility)
- Methods for integrating AI as a classroom topic
- Exchange with other teachers & real practical examples

---

- **Target group**: primary school, secondary schools
- **Duration**: from 2 hours
- **Equipment**: laptops/tablets or smartphones, Wi-Fi/internet
