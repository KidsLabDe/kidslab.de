> Material kits with everything you need for class — borrow them for free and get started.

Want to take your first steps in programming with your class yourselves? Our learning kits contain everything you need – including instructions, explainer videos, and material for immediate classroom use.

All kits can be borrowed **free of charge for up to 2 weeks** – on request, with a short introduction (approx. 30 minutes) at KidsLab.

## Robot Mouse learning kit

The kit contains **8 "Code and Go" robot mice** with all necessary accessories, instructions, and QR codes linking to videos. Perfect for a playful start into programming – with no digital devices at all!

- **Target group:** kindergarten, primary school
- **Contents:** 8 robot mice, accessories, instructions, QR codes with videos
- **Cost:** Free (made possible by the [Wilhelm-Stemmer-Stiftung](https://www.wilhelm-stemmer-stiftung.de/index.php))

More info: [Coding with the robot mouse](../roboter-maus/)

## Calliope mini learning kit

An easy introduction to the world of electronics and microcontrollers. The Calliope mini is a small computer that lets kids playfully discover the digital world – from a blinking LED to their own mini program.

The kit contains **several Calliope mini boards**, accessories, instructions, and QR codes with explainer videos and examples for immediate classroom use.

- **Target group:** primary school, from grade 3
- **Contents:** Calliope mini sets, accessories, instructions, QR codes with videos
- **Cost:** Free
