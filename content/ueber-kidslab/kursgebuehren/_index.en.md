> Fair prices, no barriers — we want every child to be able to take part.

## Nonprofit & cost-covering

KidsLab is a **nonprofit organisation**. We calculate on a cost-covering basis, without hidden fees or profit motives. Every euro flows directly back into our mission: giving children and teenagers the best possible support.


## Discounts

You can **select the 50% discount directly when booking** — just click the matching price option in the ticket shop. No proof needed, no application required.

- **50% discount** — if the regular price is too much
- **Siblings** — from the 2nd child onward: 50% off
- **OpenLab and LUGA members** — 50% supporter discount


## No child is excluded

{{< callout type="important" >}}
**If even the discounted price doesn't work, it still works out.** Just send us a quick message — by email to [team@kidslab.de](mailto:team@kidslab.de) or via [WhatsApp](https://wa.me/4982199951920). We'll find a solution, even for €0.

You don't need to prove or justify anything. Digital education shouldn't have barriers.
